# Agency Report Options Based on Current Evidence

## FTC

Best for:

- deceptive sales tactics;
- optional products presented as mandatory;
- inadequate contract disclosures;
- unfair or deceptive repossession-related conduct.

Strongest files:

- `Car Events - Google Docs.pdf`
- `CFPD Complaint Detail.pdf`
- `Gmail -Complaint Info Request CA1-FRJ-C2R.pdf`

## CFPB

Best for:

- vehicle financing and servicing;
- payment application disputes;
- loan transfer issues;
- credit reporting and insurance billing concerns.

Strongest files:

- `CFPD Complaint Detail.pdf`
- `Experian1.pdf`
- `Experian GreenRoad.pdf`
- payment and billing files.

## DFPI

Best for:

- California licensing, servicing, debt collection, and finance conduct;
- Green Road and possible On Time licensing / collection issues.

Strongest files:

- `dfpi_report_draft.md`
- `DFPI Licenseing and Info.pdf`
- `NMLS Geen Road Report  - Company.pdf`
- `Gmail -DFPI Complaint CN0019063 - DFPI Complaint Confirmation.pdf`

## California Attorney General

Best for:

- broader unfair business practices affecting consumers in California;
- deceptive add-ons, sales conduct, and unfair debt-collection or repossession patterns.

Strongest files:

- `Car Events - Google Docs.pdf`
- `CFPD Complaint Detail.pdf`
- `Green Road Finance _ Complaints _ Better Business Bureau® Profile.pdf`
- `On Time Financing _ Complaints _ Better Business Bureau® Profile.pdf`

## DMV Investigations / Occupational Licensing

Best for:

- dealer misconduct;
- title, registration, disclosure, and dealer sales practice issues;
- possible dealership handling problems involving the sale itself.

Strongest files:

- sale contract files;
- DMV files;
- `Car Events - Google Docs.pdf`

## Local District Attorney or Law Enforcement

Best for:

- only if you want to allege intentional fraud, forged signatures, knowingly false records, or criminal repossession conduct.

Current file-set limitation:

- the records show several fraud indicators, but many points still read as consumer disputes unless supported by signed contracts, ledger comparisons, title history, or direct proof of false statements.

## Best Filing Order

1. DFPI for California finance and debt-collection issues.
2. FTC for deceptive sales and unfair practices.
3. CFPB if you want a federal consumer-finance complaint trail.
4. California AG for broader unfair business practices.
5. DMV if the dealership sale, registration, title, or dealer disclosure issues are central.
