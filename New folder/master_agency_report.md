# Master Cross-Agency Report

## Purpose

This master report is designed to help submit the same core facts to multiple agencies while tailoring the framing to each agency's role.

## Main Companies Identified in the Records

- Karplus Warehouse, Inc. / Karplus Auto Sales
- Green Road Finance Inc.
- On Time Financing Inc.
- Reign Recovery / repossession company identified in the records

## Core Facts Supported by the File Set

### Sales and contract formation

- buyer sought to purchase a black 2017 Honda Civic;
- buyer states contract terms were not meaningfully explained before signing;
- buyer states optional products were presented as required, including tracking device, window etching, and extended warranty;
- buyer states there was an oral representation that no prepayment penalty would apply.

Primary files:

- `Car Events - Google Docs.pdf`
- `Car Events - Google Docs.docx`

### Payment and add-on disputes

- payments listed in the records include $4,600 on July 6, 2020, $1,000 on July 11, 2020, and $550.36 in August 2020;
- CFPB complaint states the contract showed $6,000 down payment while receipts showed $5,600;
- CFPB complaint identifies disputed add-ons including Theft Deterrent Device ($695) and Surface Protection Product ($1,495).

Primary files:

- `CFPD Complaint Detail.pdf`
- `Car Events - Google Docs.pdf`

### Loan transfer / servicing / credit reporting

- records reference Karplus, On Time Financing, and Green Road Finance;
- complaint files state the lender sequence on credit reporting was inaccurate;
- complaint files state On Time may have run credit after transfer.

Primary files:

- `CFPD Complaint Detail.pdf`

### Insurance and GAP

- consumer states Green Road and On Time redirected responsibility for GAP;

## Filing Strategy

1. Submit DFPI with the strongest finance, licensing, and collection issues.
2. Submit FTC focusing on deceptive sales and unfair practices.
3. Submit CFPB focusing on the loan, servicing, transfer, and credit reporting.
4. Submit California AG for broader unfair business practices.
5. Submit DMV if the main focus is dealer misconduct at the time of sale or title/registration irregularities.

## Important Caution

The file set contains strong indicators of deceptive and unfair conduct, but some issues still need documentary confirmation before being stated as proven fraud. The strongest approach is to phrase certain points as:

- "records indicate"
- "consumer alleges"
- "appears inconsistent with"
- "requires agency verification"

That keeps the complaint credible while still preserving the seriousness of the allegations.
