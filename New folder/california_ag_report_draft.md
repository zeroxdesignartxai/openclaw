# Draft Report to the California Attorney General Regarding Unfair and Deceptive Auto Sale, Financing, and Repossession Conduct

## Audience

California Department of Justice, Office of the Attorney General

## Scope

This report concerns the sale, financing, servicing, and repossession of a used 2017 Honda Civic involving Karplus Warehouse, Inc. / Karplus Auto Sales, Green Road Finance Inc., On Time Financing Inc., and the repossession company identified in the records as Reign Recovery.

## Executive Summary

The attached record set reflects a pattern of alleged unfair and deceptive conduct affecting a California consumer in connection with a used vehicle transaction. The evidence suggests:

- optional add-on products may have been presented as mandatory;
- contract terms may not have been properly explained before signing;
- down-payment and financed-amount figures may be inconsistent;
- tracking or starter-interrupt technology may not have been properly disclosed;
- lender-transfer and credit-reporting information may be inaccurate or misleading;
- insurance and GAP administration may have been confusing and improperly handled;
- repossession-related fees, communications, and vehicle handling may have been improper.

This complaint is appropriate for Attorney General review because it concerns potential unfair business practices, deceptive consumer-facing conduct, and a pattern of disputed auto-finance and repossession practices affecting a California resident.

## Core Allegations

### 1. Optional products may have been misrepresented as required

The records state the buyer was told that the tracking device, window etching, and extended warranty were required. The CFPB complaint also identifies charges for a Theft Deterrent Device and Surface Protection Product that the consumer states were not expressly authorized.

Evidence:

- `Car Events - Google Docs.pdf`
- `Car Events - Google Docs.docx`
- `CFPD Complaint Detail.pdf`

### 2. Material finance terms may not have been clearly disclosed

The records state the contract was not meaningfully explained and the buyer was directed where to sign. The buyer also states they were verbally told there was no prepayment penalty.

Evidence:

- `Car Events - Google Docs.pdf`
- `CFPD Complaint Detail.pdf`

### 3. Down-payment and account-balance information appears disputed

The CFPB complaint states the contract reflected a $6,000 down payment while receipts showed $5,600, and the narrative file separately lists payments of $4,600 on July 6, 2020, $1,000 on July 11, 2020, and $550.36 in August 2020.

Evidence:

- `CFPD Complaint Detail.pdf`
- `Car Events - Google Docs.pdf`

### 4. Tracking / starter-interrupt disclosure may have been inadequate

The complaint files state the buyer asked about removing a tracking device and was told it was required, while later records raise concern that tracking or starter-interrupt technology was not properly disclosed or consented to.

Evidence:

- `Car Events - Google Docs.pdf`
- `CFPD Complaint Detail.pdf`

### 5. Lender-transfer and credit-reporting history appears inconsistent

The records indicate involvement by Karplus, On Time Financing, and Green Road Finance. The consumer states credit reporting misrepresented the lender sequence and that On Time pulled credit after selling the account.

Evidence:

- `CFPD Complaint Detail.pdf`
- `Experian1.pdf`
- `Experian GreenRoad.pdf`

### 6. Insurance and GAP handling appears confusing and potentially misleading

The consumer states Green Road and On Time redirected responsibility for GAP, that Green Road referred the consumer back to Karplus for redemption, and that insurance charges varied and were not adequately explained.

Evidence:

- `CFPD Complaint Detail.pdf`
- `Portfolio Claim Summery (Sheldon).pdf`

### 7. Repossession conduct may have been unfair and damaging

The records state the vehicle was repossessed in the early morning, the repossession company changed fees and release requirements, the consumer had trouble obtaining contact information, and the vehicle was damaged in the repossession process.

Evidence:

- `Car Events - Google Docs.pdf`
- `Gmail -Complaint Info Request CA1-FRJ-C2R.pdf`
- `lyft recipt to pick up car April 27-2023.pdf`
- `Gmail - Recipt for car seat claim20003834029.pdf`

## Requested Attorney General Action

I request review of whether the companies involved engaged in unfair, deceptive, or unlawful business practices in connection with:

- sale of optional add-on products;
- disclosure of material contract and financing terms;
- disclosure of tracking or starter-interrupt technology;
- administration of insurance and GAP products;
- servicing and transfer of the loan;
- repossession-related fees, notices, and handling of the vehicle.

## Short California AG Complaint Paragraph

I am submitting a complaint regarding a used-car sale and financing transaction involving alleged deceptive add-on sales, incomplete contract disclosure, disputed down-payment and balance calculations, possible undisclosed tracking technology, confusing loan-transfer and credit-reporting history, insurance and GAP problems, and unfair repossession conduct that allegedly damaged the vehicle. I request review for possible unfair or deceptive business practices affecting a California consumer.
