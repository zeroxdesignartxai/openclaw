# CFPB Short-Form Complaint Draft

## Product

Vehicle loan or lease

## Issue

Managing the loan or lease / loan sold or transferred to another company

## Company

Green Road Finance Inc.

## What happened

I am requesting investigation of a used-vehicle financing account involving Karplus Auto Sales, On Time Financing, and Green Road Finance. My records show several problems with the origination, servicing, transfer, and repossession of this account.

At the time of sale, I was not given a meaningful explanation of the contract terms and was told that certain products were required, including a tracking device, window etching, and an extended warranty. I was also told there would be no prepayment penalty. Later, I found charges and add-on products on the contract that I do not believe I clearly authorized, including a theft deterrent device and a surface protection product.

My records also show a discrepancy in the down payment and confusion over how payments were applied to principal. I paid substantial amounts at the beginning of the transaction, but the balance did not decrease as expected. I also had problems with insurance and GAP administration, including being redirected between On Time, Green Road, and Karplus.

My credit reporting history also appears inaccurate. The lender sequence does not appear to match the actual transaction history, and I believe On Time may have pulled my credit after the account had already transferred.

The account later went into repossession. During that process, I had difficulty getting correct contact information, the repossession fees and release requirements changed repeatedly, and the vehicle was allegedly damaged during repossession. I also believe the repossession company had a duplicate key that had never been disclosed to me.

I am requesting a full payment ledger, itemized explanation of all charges, copies of all signed disclosures and add-on agreements, correction of any inaccurate credit reporting, clarification of insurance and GAP handling, and review of the repossession and transfer practices on this account.

## Desired resolution

- complete payment history and explanation of principal application;
- itemized explanation of all fees, insurance charges, and add-on charges;
- copies of all signed disclosures for tracking device, theft deterrent device, surface protection product, GAP, and warranty products;
- correction of inaccurate lender-transfer or credit-reporting information;
- explanation of repossession fees, notices, and duplicate key access;
- refund or adjustment of unauthorized or inaccurately disclosed charges.
